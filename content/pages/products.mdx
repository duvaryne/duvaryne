Alongside our AWS and DevOps consulting practice, Duvaryne builds and operates its own
SaaS products. Each one is our own IP — our idea, our engineering, our roadmap, our support. We
do not white-label them and **we do not build products to order.** They exist because we saw
Indian businesses stuck with problems nobody had solved properly for this market.

## How our products are different

1. **India-first, not India-adapted.** Designed around Indian portals, Indian regulation and the
   Indian software stack — CPPP, the DPDP Act, Razorpay, DigiLocker, Keka. Not a Western product
   with a rupee price tag bolted on.
2. **Operator-grade engineering.** We run production infrastructure for a living. Multi-tenant
   isolation, infrastructure as code, automated delivery and observability go in on day one, not
   after the first outage.
3. **Priced for the businesses that need them.** Built for MSMEs and early-stage companies priced
   out of enterprise tooling and consultants. Transparent subscription pricing, no annual lock-in
   to get started.
4. **Our own IP, our own roadmap.** Because we fund and own these products outright, we ship what
   users ask for rather than what an investor or a parent brand dictates.
5. **The people who built it are the people who answer.** A small, senior team. When you raise an
   issue it reaches an engineer who wrote the code.

## TenderCopilot — live

**Win government tenders without the guesswork.**

TenderCopilot helps Indian MSMEs bid on the Central Public Procurement Portal. It surfaces tenders
that match your business, then tells you in plain language whether you actually qualify — before
you spend days assembling a bid you were never eligible for.

### The problem we are solving

Most small Indian businesses give up on public procurement long before they lose a bid. Listings
are buried, eligibility criteria run to dozens of clauses, corrigenda change the rules mid-cycle,
and one missing document disqualifies an otherwise strong bidder. The result is a market where the
same handful of firms win repeatedly — not because they bid better, but because they can afford to
decode the process.

### What it does

- **CPPP-focused discovery.** Purpose-built for the Central Public Procurement Portal rather than a
  generic aggregator, so results match how the portal actually classifies work.
- **AI eligibility checking.** Run any tender through the checker and get a plain-language verdict:
  whether you qualify, which documents you need, and exactly where you fall short.
- **Multi-company workspaces.** Tender consultants manage several client companies from one
  account, with data isolated per company.
- **Deadline and document tracking.** EMD dates, corrigenda and submission windows in one place.

### Who it is for

Indian MSMEs that want to start bidding on central government tenders, and tender consultants
managing bids on behalf of multiple clients.

## Compliance Edge — in development

**Compliance automation built for India.**

Compliance Edge gets Indian companies audit-ready for the DPDP Act 2023, ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2
Type II and RBI cyber security frameworks — with evidence collected automatically from the tools
Indian teams already run, instead of screenshots pasted into spreadsheets.

### The problem we are solving

The DPDP Act is enforceable and enterprise buyers now ask for ISO 27001 or SOC 2 before they sign.
Indian companies are caught between consultants who bill by the month and Western compliance
platforms that do not integrate with the Indian stack or map to Indian law. Compliance becomes a
project that stalls deals rather than a control that runs quietly in the background.

### What it does

- **Indian-stack connectors.** Pulls evidence directly from Keka, GreytHR, Darwinbox, Razorpay,
  Cashfree, Zoho and DigiLocker — no manual screenshots.
- **DPDP consent and DSR engine.** Multilingual consent SDKs for web and mobile, plus an automated
  data-subject-request erasure pipeline.
- **One control set, four frameworks.** Controls mapped across DPDP, ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II and
  RBI frameworks, so evidence is collected once and reused.
- **Auditor workbench.** A dedicated portal for your CA or external auditor, backed by
  tamper-evident evidence storage.
- **Public Trust Center.** A shareable page that proves your security posture to prospects
  mid-deal.
- **Self-serve onboarding.** Connected and collecting evidence the same day.

### Who it is for

Indian startups closing enterprise deals, scale-ups facing DPDP obligations, BFSI-adjacent
companies under RBI frameworks, and the CA firms and auditors who serve them.
