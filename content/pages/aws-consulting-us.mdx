## What you are actually buying

Most AWS consulting sold to US companies is staff augmentation with a consulting invoice
attached. You buy a team, the senior name on the proposal appears at the kickoff and the
quarterly review, and the work is done by whoever is available.

This is the opposite arrangement. One principal engineer scopes the work, writes the
Terraform, and is on the call when something breaks. That constrains how much we can take on
at once, which is a real limitation and worth knowing before you start. It also means there is
no delivery pyramid built into the price.

## Fixed price, in dollars, agreed before we start

Every engagement is quoted as a fixed USD figure against a written scope. No hourly meters, no
discovery phase you pay for, no invoice that arrives larger than the conversation implied.

If we scope it wrong, we absorb it. If you change what you want, we price the change separately
and you agree it in writing first. Engagements generally start at $15,000.

We will not take a percentage of savings on cost work. That model rewards finding numbers that
look good in one quarterly dashboard rather than ones that survive a year, and it puts us on the
wrong side of the table on the day the honest answer is "leave this alone".

## Compliance-driven infrastructure

If you are heading into SOC 2, ISO 27001 or a HIPAA assessment, most of what an auditor examines
in your AWS account is infrastructure work that should have been done anyway: least-privilege IAM
rather than broad managed policies, encryption in transit and at rest with keys you control,
signed container images that fail admission when unverified, centralised audit logging, and every
control defined as code so the evidence is reproducible rather than screenshotted.

We are not auditors and do not issue attestations. We build the layer the attestation is about.

## What you own at the end

Everything is Infrastructure as Code, in your repository, in your AWS account. No proprietary
tooling, no managed console you rent from us, no lock-in by design. If you take it in-house in a
year, the handover is a `git clone` and a walkthrough.

That is deliberate, and it is the part most consultancies quietly omit. A platform only one
vendor understands is a liability on your balance sheet, not an asset.

## Working across timezones

Our day overlaps the US morning. Calls are scheduled in your business hours. The default mode is
asynchronous and written — scopes, findings documents, pull requests you review when it suits
you — which is how the work stays reviewable rather than dependent on being in the same room.

If what you need is someone physically in a US timezone for daily standups, say so on the first
call and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.
