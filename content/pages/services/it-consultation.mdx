## What IT consulting actually gets you

IT consulting is an assessment of where your technology is today and a plan for where it needs
to be. A useful engagement produces decisions — not a deck. Ours covers infrastructure and
application review, a cloud adoption or modernisation roadmap, a security and compliance gap
analysis, a cost review, and a prioritised delivery plan with owners and dates against each item.

The reason to start here rather than with a build is economics. A two-week assessment that tells
you a rewrite is unnecessary is worth considerably more than a six-month rewrite that nobody
questioned.

## Where we do the work

### IT strategy and roadmap

We assess the current landscape and produce a roadmap you can actually finance: what to fix now,
what to defer, what to stop paying for, and what to leave alone. Sequenced against your business
calendar rather than a vendor's.

### Cloud, Infrastructure as Code and modernisation

Terraform and Ansible for infrastructure, in your repository, reviewed like application code. The
value is not that the infrastructure is scripted; it is that infrastructure changes become diffs a
second engineer can read before they reach production, and a rebuilt environment is identical to
the one it replaced.

Most legacy systems do not need a rewrite. Containerising the existing application, moving the
database to a managed service, and putting a proper delivery pipeline around it usually removes
the majority of the operational pain at a fraction of the cost — and it is reversible, which a
rewrite is not.

### Security and compliance

We handle the technical control side: access management, encryption in transit and at rest,
logging and audit trails, backup and disaster recovery, vulnerability scanning, and the evidence
your auditor will ask for. For ISO 27001, SOC 2 and DPDP work we operate alongside your compliance
advisor rather than replacing them — that division of labour is cheaper and produces better
evidence than either party attempting both.

### Managed IT support

Once it is live, someone has to hold it. Monitoring, alerting, patching and cost review on a
monthly retainer, including support for distributed and remote teams. Either as your entire
platform function or alongside engineers you already have.

## How we price it

Short fixed-scope assessments are the most predictable way to buy consulting. A one-week
production readiness review or a two-week cost optimisation sprint is quoted as a single fixed fee,
agreed in writing before work starts. Longer programmes are quoted per phase. Fractional retainers
are priced on a fixed number of hours per month.

No hourly meters, and no scope that quietly expands.

## Who this is a good fit for

- Startups and SMBs that need senior architectural judgement without carrying a senior salary.
- Teams inheriting infrastructure nobody documented, who need to know what they actually have.
- Companies facing an audit who need the technical evidence to exist before the auditor arrives.
- Engineering leaders who want a second senior opinion before committing to a large build.

## Who it is not

If you need desktop support, procurement or a helpdesk, we are the wrong firm and will say so on
the first call. We work on cloud infrastructure, delivery pipelines and the platforms underneath
applications — not end-user IT.
