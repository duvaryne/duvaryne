## A specialist consultancy, deliberately small

Duvaryne LLP is an AWS and DevOps consultancy based in Bengaluru, Karnataka. We are
DPIIT-recognised, we work across India, and we work remotely with engineering teams in the US and
EU.

We are small on purpose. Most consulting engagements fail in one of two ways: a strategy deck
arrives and nothing gets built, or juniors are parachuted in behind a senior name on the pitch. The
structure below is the fix for both — the person who reviews your account is the person who does
the work.

Thirteen years of hands-on production infrastructure: cloud migration, container platforms,

with traffic.

**Credentials, all verifiable:**

- **Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA)** — the CNCF-administered, hands-on examination for

- **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate** — AWS architecture, security and cost design.
- **Professional Certificate in DevOps, IIT Madras** — formal training in CI/CD, infrastructure as

Find the work at [GitHub](https://github.com/duvaryne) and the professional record on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/duvaryne).

Naming the engineer matters more than it looks. A consultancy attributable to nobody is one you
cannot check. Everything on this site — every architecture decision, every number — traces back to
a named person who can be asked about it.

## What we have actually delivered

Our case studies document measurable outcomes rather than adjectives. Compute spend cut 50–60% on a
production EKS estate using Karpenter with Spot consolidation. Node provisioning reduced from
roughly four minutes to about forty-five seconds. A recovery time objective under fifteen minutes
for multi-region failover, verified by an actual region-failover test rather than a document.
Cryptographic image attestation with Cosign and Kyverno that blocks unsigned artifacts at the
cluster boundary. A ninety percent reduction in after-hours alerts.

## How an engagement runs

**Scope is written and priced fixed before work begins.** No hourly meters, no discovery phase you
pay for, no scope that quietly expands.

**Delivery is embedded.** We build the pipeline and the infrastructure alongside your engineers and
document it as we go, because a platform only one consultancy understands is a liability rather
than an asset.

**Everything is Infrastructure as Code, in your repository, under your account.** There is no
lock-in to us, by design. If you want to take it in-house in a year, the handover is a `git clone`.

**Handover or retainer — your call.** Where there is no platform engineer to hand over to yet, we
stay on a monthly retainer as a fractional platform function. That is often the right answer for a
team of eight to twenty engineers: senior platform capability without carrying a senior platform
salary before you need to.

## Who we are a good fit for

- Startups that need a production-ready AWS foundation without hiring a platform team yet.
- Companies whose AWS bill has grown faster than their traffic and who want to know why.
- Teams facing an audit — SOC 2, ISO 27001, DPDP — who need the infrastructure evidence to exist.
- Engineering leaders who want a second senior opinion before committing to an architecture.

## Who we are not a fit for

If you run three services with predictable traffic and a bill under a few hundred dollars a month,
you probably do not need us yet — and we will tell you that on the call rather than sell you a
landing zone.

If you are looking for the cheapest possible hourly rate, we will not be it. If you need someone to
own the outcome without involving your engineers, we are the wrong shape; embedded delivery is how
we work and it requires your team's time.

And if we read your account and find nothing meaningful to recover, we will say so. It happens.
Saying it is worth more to us than a small engagement.
